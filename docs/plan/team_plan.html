EfxINNOs Hackathon Romania · Challenge 04 Ecosystem Health

Black Sea Health Viewer: build plan

A map-first viewer, like Copernicus MyOcean Viewer, for the Romanian coast. It adds long-term trends, in-situ points, anomalies against reference conditions, and the pressure zones of the five blue-economy sectors. It opens with a short questionnaire so a beach manager, a student and a NIMRD researcher each get a view that fits them.

3 · Science

Indicators and how we answer the jury

IndicatorBest sourceReferenceWatch / alert (draft, to confirm with NIMRD)
Thermal stressSatellite SSTDay-of-year climatology 1991–2020z > 2 / above p90 for 5+ days (marine heatwave definition)
OxygenBuoys, then modelEMODnet seasonal climatology< 4 mg/L / < 2 mg/L (hypoxia)
EutrophicationSatellite chl (log scale)Satellite climatology 1998–2020z > 2 / > 10 mg/m³
Water clarityKd490, SPM, buoy turbiditySatellite climatologyz > 2 / persistent for 7+ days
Driver: Danube dischargeGloFAS1997–2025 mean, about 6,500 m³/sContext only: explains natural changes
  • Natural variability vs real change: anomalies are always measured against the same day of year, never the yearly mean. Upwelling and Danube flood flags explain events, they don't count as damage.
  • Integration: sources on different grids are compared at the same point and on the same daily step. The origin label and the pixel actually used are always shown.
  • Uncertainty: confidence goes up when sources agree. The jury explicitly asks whether a value is measured directly or derived.
  • Ecological meaning: an anomaly triggers an "investigate" suggestion (sample oxygen, check a ROV transect), not a verdict.

NOAA servers time out on long requestsRequests are already split into 40-day chunks and cached
No official aquaculture or offshore-platform locationsResearch in progress; anything assumed is marked as assumed on the map
Thresholds questioned by the juryShow the reference period and source for every threshold; ask NIMRD lecturers to confirm before the pitch
Too many features, nothing finishedMust-have for the demo: questionnaire, map with zones and in-situ points, one comparison chart with an anomaly band, one zone health card. Everything else is a bonus
